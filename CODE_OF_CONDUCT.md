# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that help build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with other people.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting useful feedback with grace.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community, not only for ourselves.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, or attacks on a person or their beliefs.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as an address or email, without their clear permission.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

The maintainer explains and enforces these standards. The maintainer will take fair action in response to any behaviour that breaks them.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when it is appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in a public space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@niihhhall](https://github.com/niihhhall), through the [Security tab](https://github.com/niihhhall/gogreen-mvp/security) (**Report a vulnerability**) of this repository. Write "Code of Conduct" at the start of the title.

All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reports.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides the response, from a private warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the project, depending on how serious the behaviour is and whether it repeats.
